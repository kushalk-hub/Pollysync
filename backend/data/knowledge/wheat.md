# Wheat Farming Best Practices

## Planting
- Optimal planting time: November-December in Maharashtra
- Seed rate: 100-125 kg/ha
- Seed depth: 5-6 cm
- Row spacing: 20-23 cm
- Soil preparation: Fine tilth, well-leveled

## Water Management
- Critical stages: Crown root, Tillering, Flowering, Grain filling
- 4-6 irrigations recommended
- First irrigation at 21-25 days after sowing
- Avoid waterlogging at any stage

## Nutrient Management
- Apply 120 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O per hectare
- Split nitrogen: 50% at sowing, 25% at first irrigation, 25% at second irrigation
- Zinc sulphate application if deficient

## Pest Management
- Common pests: Termites, Aphids, Rust
- Use resistant varieties
- Apply carbosulfan for termites
- Monitor for rust symptoms

## Harvesting
- Harvest when grain moisture is 14-15%
- Combine harvesting when crop is fully mature
- Store in clean, dry bins

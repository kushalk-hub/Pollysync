# Pollination Best Practices

## Importance of Pollination
- 75% of crop species depend on pollinators
- Pollination increases yield by 20-30%
- Improves crop quality and uniformity
- Essential for seed production in many crops

## Supporting Pollinators
- Plant pollinator-friendly border crops
- Avoid pesticide spraying during flowering
- Maintain natural habitats near farms
- Provide nesting sites for wild bees

## Bee-Friendly Practices
- Provide water sources for bees
- Grow diverse flowering plants
- Use integrated pest management
- Plant cover crops that support pollinators

## Managing Bee Hives
- Place hives near crop fields (within 500m)
- Ensure 2-3 hives per hectare for effective pollination
- Monitor hive health regularly
- Provide supplementary feeding during dearth periods

## Crop-Specific Pollination
- Mustard: Self-pollinating but benefits from bee visits
- Sunflower: Cross-pollinating, requires bee activity
- Cotton: Primarily wind-pollinated, but bees improve yield
- Wheat: Self-pollinating, minimal bee dependency

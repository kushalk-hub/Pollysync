# Cotton Farming Best Practices

## Planting
- Optimal planting time: May-June (Kharif)
- Seed rate: 5-7 kg/ha
- Row spacing: 90-120 cm
- Plant spacing: 45-60 cm
- Seed depth: 2-3 cm

## Water Management
- Critical stages: Flowering and boll development
- Total water requirement: 700-1300 mm
- Irrigate at 50-60% depletion of available soil moisture
- Stop irrigation 2-3 weeks before harvest

## Nutrient Management
- Apply 120 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 60 kg K2O per hectare
- Split nitrogen: 50% at sowing, 50% at flowering
- Micronutrient application as needed

## Pest Management
- Common pests: Bollworm, Aphids, Jassids
- Use Bt cotton varieties where available
- Install pheromone traps for bollworm
- Practice integrated pest management

## Harvesting
- Pick cotton when bolls are fully open
- Multiple pickings may be needed
- Store in clean, dry bags
- Avoid contamination with other fibers

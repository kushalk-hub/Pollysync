# Sunflower Farming Best Practices

## Planting
- Optimal planting time: October-November (Rabi) or June-July (Kharif)
- Seed rate: 8-10 kg/ha
- Row spacing: 60 cm
- Plant spacing: 30 cm
- Seed depth: 3-4 cm

## Water Management
- Critical stages: Flowering and seed filling
- Total water requirement: 400-600 mm
- Irrigate at 50% field capacity
- Avoid waterlogging

## Nutrient Management
- Apply 80 kg N, 40 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O per hectare
- Boron application improves seed set
- Sulphur deficiency common, apply 20 kg S/ha

## Pest Management
- Common pests: Gall midge, Capitulum borer
- Use pheromone traps for monitoring
- Apply chlorantraniliprole for borer control
- Avoid broad-spectrum insecticides

## Harvesting
- Harvest when back of flower head turns yellow-brown
- Dry seeds to 9% moisture before storage
- Store in cool, dry place

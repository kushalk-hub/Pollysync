# Mustard Farming Best Practices

## Planting
- Optimal planting time: October-November in Maharashtra
- Seed rate: 5-6 kg/ha
- Row spacing: 30 cm
- Seed depth: 2-3 cm
- Soil temperature: 18-25°C for germination

## Water Management
- Critical stages: flowering and seed filling
- Irrigate at 25-30% soil moisture depletion
- Total water requirement: 300-400 mm
- Avoid waterlogging during flowering

## Pest Management
- Common pests: Aphids, Sawfly, Painted bug
- Use yellow sticky traps for monitoring
- Apply neem oil spray for aphid control
- Avoid broad-spectrum pesticides during flowering

## Nutrient Management
- Apply 60 kg N, 30 kg P2O5, 30 kg K2O per hectare
- Split nitrogen: 50% at sowing, 50% at flowering
- Sulphur application improves oil content

## Harvesting
- Harvest when 75-80% pods turn brown
- Optimal moisture content: 8-10%
- Avoid harvesting during rain
- Store in cool, dry place

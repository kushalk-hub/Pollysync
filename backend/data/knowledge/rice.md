# Rice Farming Best Practices

## Planting
- Optimal planting time: June-July (Kharif)
- Seed rate: 20-25 kg/ha (transplanting)
- Row spacing: 20 cm
- Plant spacing: 15 cm
- Seed depth: 1-2 cm

## Water Management
- Critical stages: Tillering, Flowering, Grain filling
- Maintain 5-7 cm standing water during vegetative stage
- Drain field 1-2 weeks before harvest
- Alternate wetting and drying saves water

## Nutrient Management
- Apply 120 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 60 kg K2O per hectare
- Split nitrogen: 50% at sowing, 25% at tillering, 25% at panicle initiation
- Zinc application commonly needed

## Pest Management
- Common pests: Stem borer, Leaf folder, Brown planthopper
- Use resistant varieties
- Install light traps for monitoring
- Apply neem-based pesticides as first option

## Harvesting
- Harvest when 80% of panicles turn golden
- Grain moisture should be 20-25%
- Dry to 14% moisture for storage
- Store in clean, dry granaries
